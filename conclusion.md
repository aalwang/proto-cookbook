Conclusion:

In this cookbook CMIP6 historical total precipitation data from 5 model members was loaded into our environment from the cloud using Intake-ESM. This data was subset for the the time period 1950-2009 over a region that roughly follows what is usually referred to as Oceania. Various visualizations were created to illustrate averages and trends in precipitation over this region using Matplotlib and Cartopy. After evaluating the CMIP dataset, in another notebook the accuracy of the CMIP6 model output was tested against ERA5 reanalysis data. ERA5 data was accessed via the Destination Earth data server. 

This cookbook has yet to explore in detail the physical reasoning behind any trends and/or discrepancies in the CMIP6/ERA5 preciptation data. Significant additions must be made to this cookbook to more completely evaluate the accuracy of historical CMIP6 runs in Oceania.
